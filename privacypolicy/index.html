Effective Date: April 17, 2026

At Ware's Media, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and share your data when you interact with our website and book our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our website. Specifically, when you submit a call booking form, we collect the following:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly to facilitate our professional media services and streamline our communications with you. We use your data to:

  • Route your contact details into our internal CRM systems (such as Pipedrive) to manage your inquiry. (Please note: Ware's Media uses CRM platforms strictly for internal lead management; we do not sell or provide CRM management subscriptions to our clients).
  • Follow up and set up scheduled calls.
  • Send you email listings and relevant communications regarding your inquiry.
3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share your data only with specific, trusted third-party platforms that are essential for our internal business operations and marketing optimization:

  • Internal Business Tools: We utilize internal CRM systems to securely store your contact information and manage our project pipelines.
  • Analytics and Advertising Partners: We share data with platforms such as Meta and Google. This allows us to analyze user interactions, track the effectiveness of our campaigns, and adjust our systems to optimize conversions.
4. Data Retention and Security

We implement reasonable security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information when you enter, submit, or access your data. We retain your information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy or to comply with legal obligations.

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data we have collected about you. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.
